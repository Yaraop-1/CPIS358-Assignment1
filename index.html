THE PROBLEM

The problem we want to solve

In many ports, information about ships and containers is spread over phone calls, paper forms and separate systems. Everyone who works with a container needs the same answer — where is it and what is its status? — but each person has to ask somebody else.

  • Customers cannot see where their cargo is, so they call the port again and again.
  • Customs brokers learn about holds late, which delays the clearance.
  • Managers have no single view of ships, containers and customs holds.
THE SOLUTION

ABHARNA: one gateway for the whole port

ABHARNA is the digital chart that brings the operations of the port into a single, transparent view. It works in four simple steps:

  1. Port staff register the ships and containers.
  2. Every movement updates the container status (registered, in transit, at port, customs hold, cleared, released).
  3. Customers track their cargo with only the container number.
  4. Customs brokers and managers see holds and clearance in the same place.
